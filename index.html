The Current Challenge

Manual Efficiency

Case allocation and tracking are done manually via Excel, leading to errors and delays.

Black Box Ops

Limited visibility into what Debt Collection Agencies (DCAs) are actually doing.

Audit Risks

Minimal audit trails and unclear ownership of overdue accounts.

System Architecture

Layer 1: RPA

The Data Bridge

Bots scrape legacy systems for overdue accounts and auto-update internal ledgers. Zero manual entry.

Layer 2: AI Core

Intelligence

Analyzes payment history to assign a "Propensity to Pay" score. Moves from random allocation to strategic prioritization.

Layer 3: Portal

Engagement

A secure web dashboard where DCAs view cases and log actions. Real-time insights.

Governance & Compliance

  • SOP Enforcement
    The portal enforces workflow. Agents cannot skip steps (e.g., must upload "Notice 1" before "Call 2").
  • Audit Trail
    Every click and action is timestamped, ensuring 100% accountability.
  • Role-Based Access
    Secure portals ensure DCAs only see specific data relevant to their cases.
